## The Challenge

Rocket Loans needed to establish organic visibility in the highly competitive personal loan market while maintaining strict compliance with federal and state lending regulations. The personal loan space is dominated by aggressive paid advertising, making cost-effective lead acquisition critical for profitability.

### Key Challenges

- Extremely competitive keywords (CPCs $40-$80 for personal loan terms)
- Complex compliance requirements (APR disclosure, state restrictions, TILA)
- Building trust in unsecured lending space
- High lead costs through traditional channels ($92/lead)
- Differentiating from predatory lenders and competitors

## Our Strategy

We developed a compliance-first SEO strategy that balanced aggressive lead generation with regulatory requirements and trust-building.

### 1. Compliance-Optimized Content

- **Regulatory Pages**: Created state-specific landing pages with proper licensing and APR disclosures
- **Transparent Calculators**: Built compliant loan calculators with full rate disclosure
- **Educational Content**: Developed 180+ guides on responsible borrowing and debt consolidation
- **Comparison Tools**: Created "Rocket Loans vs alternatives" pages with fair comparison framework

### 2. High-Intent Keyword Targeting

- Focused on qualified searches: "personal loans [credit score]", "debt consolidation loans"
- Captured "quick approval" and "same-day funding" search intent
- Targeted long-tail keywords: "personal loans for home improvement", "medical emergency loans"
- Developed content for different credit tiers (excellent, good, fair)

### 3. Trust & Authority Building

- **Customer Reviews**: Grew from 3,200 to 47,000+ verified reviews
- **Third-Party Validation**: Secured editorial mentions in Forbes, NerdWallet, Bankrate
- **Expert Content**: Published financial advice from licensed advisors
- **Security Features**: Highlighted encryption, data protection, and BBB rating

### 4. Conversion Optimization

- **Simplified Application**: Reduced form friction from 12 fields to 4 initial fields
- **Real-Time Decisions**: Prominently featured fast approval messaging
- **Mobile Experience**: Optimized for mobile applications (72% of traffic)
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Implemented multi-step forms with higher completion rates
