## The Challenge

The U.S. Small Business Administration's website serves as the primary digital resource for America's 33 million small businesses seeking loans, grants, counseling, and disaster assistance. Despite having comprehensive content, SBA.gov suffered from poor search visibility, accessibility issues, and outdated information architecture preventing small business owners from finding critical resources.

### Core Issues

- Massive site with 14,000+ pages but low organic visibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance failures affecting disabled entrepreneurs
- Outdated content architecture from 2008 redesign
- Government bureaucracy creating content approval bottlenecks
- Mobile experience failing 68% of small business owners accessing via phone
- Critical pages (loan applications, disaster relief) ranking behind private lenders

## Our Approach

We implemented a comprehensive government-compliant SEO strategy prioritizing accessibility, mobile experience, and helping small businesses find the right programs.

### 1. Accessibility-First Redesign

- **WCAG 2.1 AAA Compliance**: Achieved highest accessibility standard across all pages
- **Screen Reader Optimization**: Restructured content for assistive technology users
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Ensured full site navigation without mouse
- **Color Contrast**: Redesigned all visual elements for vision-impaired users
- **Plain Language**: Rewrote complex government content in accessible language

### 2. Information Architecture Overhaul

- **User Research**: Conducted studies with 340 small business owners to understand search behavior
- **Content Categorization**: Restructured 14,000+ pages into intuitive service categories
- **Navigation Redesign**: Simplified menu from 7 levels to 3 for easier wayfinding
- **Internal Linking**: Created strategic linking between related programs and services
- **Breadcrumbs**: Implemented consistent breadcrumb navigation across all pages

### 3. Mobile-First Optimization

- **Responsive Design**: Rebuilt pages to work seamlessly on all devices
- **Touch Optimization**: Redesigned forms and CTAs for finger navigation
- **Page Speed**: Reduced mobile load times from 8.4s to 1.7s
- **Progressive Web App**: Enabled offline access to critical resources
- **Mobile Forms**: Simplified loan and grant applications for mobile completion

### 4. Strategic Content Optimization

- **Keyword Research**: Identified 8,400+ searches small business owners use (not government terminology)
- **Program Pages**: Rewrote 840+ program descriptions in plain language with clear eligibility
- **Local Content**: Created city-specific pages for SBA district offices and resources
- **FAQ Optimization**: Structured 2,400+ FAQs to capture featured snippets
- **Multilingual SEO**: Optimized Spanish-language content for Hispanic business owners

### 5. Technical SEO Foundation

- **Schema Markup**: Implemented Government, Organization, and FAQPage schema
- **Site Speed**: Optimized all assets, achieving sub-2-second load times
- **Security**: Enhanced HTTPS implementation and security headers
- **XML Sitemaps**: Created intelligent sitemaps for better indexing
- **404 Recovery**: Built redirect strategy for 3,200+ broken government URLs
