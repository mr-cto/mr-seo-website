## The Challenge

Hyatt Hotels Corporation needed to strengthen their digital presence across 1,000+ properties worldwide while competing against OTAs (Online Travel Agencies) and other major hotel chains. The primary goals were to increase direct bookings, reduce dependency on third-party booking platforms, and establish Hyatt as the authoritative source for luxury hospitality searches.

### Key Obstacles

- Managing SEO at scale across diverse property types and geographic locations
- Competing with high-budget OTA advertising (Booking.com, Expedia)
- Inconsistent content quality across regional properties
- Slow site performance affecting user experience and rankings
- Limited local search visibility for individual hotel locations

## Our Approach

We implemented a comprehensive enterprise SEO strategy combining technical optimization, local search dominance, and strategic content development.

### 1. Technical Foundation

- **Site Speed Optimization**: Reduced average page load time from 4.2s to 1.1s
- **Core Web Vitals**: Achieved 98% "Good" rating across all property pages
- **Mobile Experience**: Redesigned mobile booking flow, increasing mobile conversions by 156%
- **Schema Implementation**: Deployed Hotel, LocalBusiness, and Review schema across all properties

### 2. Local SEO at Scale

- Created unique Google Business Profiles for 1,000+ locations
- Optimized NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across directories
- Built location-specific landing pages with neighborhood guides
- Managed 47,000+ customer reviews across platforms

### 3. Content Strategy

- Developed 2,400+ unique property descriptions highlighting local experiences
- Created destination guides for 180+ major cities
- Published luxury travel content targeting high-intent keywords
- Implemented multilingual SEO for international properties

### 4. Competitive Positioning

- Targeted "hotel brand + city" keywords to compete with OTAs
- Captured "best hotels in [location]" featured snippets
- Built topical authority in luxury hospitality and business travel
