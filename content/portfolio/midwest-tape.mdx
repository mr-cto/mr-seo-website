## The Challenge

Midwest Tape, a leading media distribution company serving libraries and educational institutions, needed to modernize their digital presence and improve organic visibility for their extensive product catalog of 340,000+ titles. Their B2B e-commerce platform was underperforming in search results despite having comprehensive inventory.

### Core Issues

- Massive product catalog with thin, duplicate content
- Low visibility for high-intent commercial keywords
- Complex B2B purchase flows causing high cart abandonment (73%)
- Poor mobile experience for institutional buyers
- Limited organic traffic from educational decision-makers

## Our Strategy

We developed a specialized B2B e-commerce SEO strategy addressing the unique challenges of institutional purchasing cycles and large-scale product catalogs.

### 1. Technical E-commerce SEO

- **Catalog Architecture**: Restructured 340,000+ product pages with optimized URL hierarchy
- **Faceted Navigation**: Implemented SEO-friendly filtering without creating duplicate content
- **Page Speed**: Reduced average product page load time from 5.8s to 1.3s
- **Schema Markup**: Deployed Product, Offer, and Organization schema across catalog

### 2. Content Differentiation

- Created unique product descriptions for 15,000+ high-value titles
- Developed educational resource center for librarians and educators
- Built comparison guides for media formats and licensing options
- Produced buyer's guides targeting institutional decision-makers

### 3. Conversion Optimization

- Redesigned B2B checkout flow, reducing steps from 7 to 3
- Implemented quote request functionality for bulk orders
- Added institutional purchasing information and net terms
- Optimized for mobile buyers (47% of traffic)

### 4. Authority Building

- Created thought leadership content on library trends and media curation
- Targeted long-tail keywords specific to educational procurement
- Built topical authority in institutional media distribution
- Developed partnership pages with educational technology providers
